How the radar prices a token that does not exist yet

The comps model

For each category, the radar takes the median market-cap / TVL ratio of the tokenized protocols in that category — what the market already pays per dollar of TVL for a token in the same line of business. A category with fewer than 3 tokenized comps falls back to the global median ratio. Then:

implied value = TVL × category ratio × growth factor × age factor × chain factor

  • Growth factor = 1 + clamp(7d change, −50%, +100%) ÷ 400. A +100% week adds 25%; a −50% week cuts 12.5%. No 7d figure means 1.00.
  • Age factor = 0.90 + min(age, 5y) ÷ 5y × 0.20, so 0.90–1.10. Older, longer-tracked protocols score slightly higher. No listing date means 1.00.
  • Chain factor = 1 + 0.02 × (chains − 1), capped at 1.10. Multi-chain reach is worth a little, not a lot.

Rows sort by implied value, ties broken by TVL then slug, and implied values are forced strictly decreasing down the ranks so rank 1 is always the most valuable.

What the board covers

The board holds 198 rows: all 40 protocols on the snapshot's watchlist (TVL ≥ $50M, records no token, not a wrapped, staked or bridged product) plus the next 158 pre-token protocols by TVL that pass the same screen — no bridge, liquid-staking, restaked, or exchange product ranks as a launch candidate. The watchlist is a floor, never a ceiling — the radar exists to find the early ones below it.

What it does not claim

DefiLlama's record is not proof. A protocol records no token; that is not a promise it has none, and it is not a promise it will ever issue one. No airdrop is confirmed anywhere on this page. Farm steps are generic on-chain actions with a cost and a time each; verify any launch only through the protocol's official channels, and never pay anyone for an early token.
